## Recharge Android Terms

Recharge gives a cardiovascular training estimate from your Health Connect data. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Talk with a qualified health professional before making medical decisions.

Estimates depend on data shared by compatible fitness sources. They do not measure muscle repair, illness, injury risk or whether you are fit to train. One-minute heart rate recovery is unavailable. Wear OS and widgets are not included.

Recharge+ adds personal baselines, overnight context, calibration, intensity corrections and pinned recovery times. Google Play shows the current price, billing period and any eligible trial. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions. Lifetime access is a one-time purchase. Google Play handles payments, cancellations and refund requests.

No account is required. Contact [jackwallner@gmail.com](mailto:jackwallner@gmail.com).
