## Recharge Android Privacy Policy

Recharge reads workouts, heart rate, active energy, resting heart rate, HRV (RMSSD), sleep, breathing rate, VO2 max and weight from Health Connect with your permission. Health readings, effort answers and derived estimates stay on your device. We do not send health data to a server. Read access is optional per data type and can be revoked in Health Connect. Core workout revocation clears the local cache. Optional revocation removes the affected inputs and recalculates local estimates. Delete local health data clears the cache without deleting records from Health Connect. Removing app storage also deletes it. RevenueCat and Google Play process purchase information, anonymous app identifiers, app version information, and counts, dates and short labels about purchase screens. No health data or free text is included in purchase diagnostics. No ads or app account. Contact jackwallner@gmail.com.
