Privacy Policy

Recharge reads workouts, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, and active energy from Apple Health, only after you grant permission. The app never writes data to Apple Health.

All health data, recovery estimates, and the answers you give the app are processed and stored locally on your iPhone and Apple Watch. They are not sold, not used for advertising, and never uploaded to our servers. There is no account and no sign-in.

RevenueCat and Apple process the purchase information needed to provide the optional Recharge Pro unlock. No health data is sent to RevenueCat.

Recovery estimates are a cardiovascular training aid for your own awareness. They are not medical advice and do not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition.
