Recharge Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Recharge reads selected Apple Health data only after you grant permission. It does not require an account and does not write to Apple Health.

Health and profile data

The app may read workouts, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, sleep, active energy, date of birth, and biological sex to calculate a training and recovery estimate. Health data, estimates, and answers you enter are processed and stored locally on your iPhone and Apple Watch. They are not uploaded, sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties. Revoke access in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Recharge+ purchases are processed by Apple and entitlement status is verified by RevenueCat. RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user ID, purchase and entitlement information, and limited technical context. It does not receive your HealthKit data, estimates, or answers. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it or remove the app. Apple and RevenueCat retain transaction records under their policies. Recharge is not affiliated with Garmin, WHOOP, Oura, Polar, Coros, or Apple.

Children and changes

Recharge is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We may update this policy when the app or its services change.
