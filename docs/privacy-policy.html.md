## Recharge Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Recharge reads selected Apple Health data only after you grant
permission. It does not require an account and does not write to Apple
Health.

## Health and profile data

The app may read workouts, workout heart rate, resting heart rate,
heart-rate variability, sleep, active energy, respiratory rate,
one-minute heart-rate recovery, cardio fitness (VO2 max), body mass,
date of birth, and biological sex to calculate a cardiovascular
training-load recovery estimate. Recharge may derive a session load,
observed maximum heart rate, training level, recovery window, and
personal recovery factor from those readings. Health data, estimates,
and answers you enter are processed and stored locally on your iPhone
and Apple Watch. Recharge does not upload Health data, use it for
advertising or marketing, sell it, or share it with third parties.
Revoking access in iOS Settings stops new reads; use Settings in
Recharge to delete its local cache.

## Purchases

Recharge+ purchases are processed by Apple and entitlement status is
verified by RevenueCat. RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user ID,
purchase and entitlement information, and limited technical context to
provide purchases and restore access. It does not receive your HealthKit
data, estimates, or answers. See
[RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

## Retention and deletion

Recharge stores its local cache in an App Group excluded from device
backup. You can delete imported workouts, estimates, snapshots, and
Health-derived profile values from Settings › Delete local health data,
or remove the app. This does not delete records from Apple Health and
does not delete Apple or RevenueCat transaction records. Apple and
RevenueCat retain transaction records under their policies.

## Children and changes

Recharge is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly
collect personal information from children. We may update this policy
when the app or its services change.
