Recharge: Recovery Time

Garmin-style recovery time, on the Apple Watch you already own.

Finish a hard session and Recharge starts a countdown. When it runs out, you get a clear Ready. That is the whole app — the answer a Garmin gives you after an activity, built on Apple Health. No ring, no strap, no second hardware ecosystem to subscribe to.

Built for hybrid training

Most recovery apps run one universal number. Recharge runs a separate curve for each kind of session, because an hour under a barbell and an hour on the bike do not cost the same thing.

Endurance

Run, ride, row, swim. Heart-rate-reserve load from your workout heart rate.

Strength

Wrist heart rate is unreliable under grip and bar contact, so Recharge asks you one question instead of guessing.

Mixed

HYROX, CrossFit, circuits. Both systems taxed, so the longest window.

Easy

Walks, yoga, mobility. Active recovery — never starts or extends a countdown.

On your watch face

Four complication families — circular, rectangular, inline, and corner — in the style you pick: the hours countdown, the estimated ready time, or just the state.

Honest by design

  • A bounded window, not a false-precision number.
  • It tells you when it is still building your baseline.
  • It shows which signal produced the estimate, and how confident it is.
  • When a countdown ends it asks how you actually felt, and tunes your own bands from your answer.

Private

Recharge reads from Apple Health and writes nothing back. Your workouts, heart data, and answers stay on your devices. No account, no sign-in, no server.

Recharge gives a cardiovascular training estimate based on your recent workout load. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Garmin, WHOOP, Oura, Polar, Coros, or Apple.
