## Good moments for the star prompt

1. A countdown reaches Ready and no readiness question is owed (the free tier, which is never asked the question). Ready is the one thing the app exists to produce, and the user is looking at it.
2. The readiness question is answered "Felt ready" (Recharge+). The clearest good result the app records; the sheet has just closed.

Why not more: "Okay but not fresh" and "Not ready" are not good results, so they never ask. A Ready with the question still owed does not ask either, because the answer is the next moment; if the user dismisses the question without answering, nothing asks until the next Ready. The first session cannot ask (2nd cold launch gate), onboarding cannot ask (`hasCompletedSetup`), and nothing asks after a paywall, a purchase sheet, an error or a failed load because no such path posts a moment.

Trigger audit before the change: Ready moment (kept, now with the gates above) and `evaluateLaunchSurfaces` calling the review ask for "engaged use" (6 launches, 7 days; a launch trigger, removed). Settings' Rate row is a manual link, kept. No other `requestReview()` calls. No analytics capture call exists in this app, so there is no `rating_asked` event.

Behaviour changes worth knowing: the gate used to need 2 Ready moments, 3 launches and 3 days; it is now the 2nd launch and the first good moment. Installs that sent feedback before (`submittedFeedback`) can be asked again. Installs asked within the last 120 days can be asked again after 30.

## Held for Jack

- **`trial_cta` arms `my` and `your`.** Not shipped. This app's trial buttons deliberately read "Continue with Recharge+" with no trial or price words (`onboarding-and-pitch.md`, Apple 3.1.2(c): the billed amount must stay the largest pricing element). "Start my free trial" on the button is a change to that decision, and each arm needs 49 translations. The reader, the switch, the routing and the attribute are in, so adding an arm is one enum case, its copy and its translations. The result line says `trialcta=held` for this reason.
- Nothing else touches how membership is judged, when a paywall or trial appears, prices or onboarding order. The trial offer is still evaluated after a Ready exactly as before (not after a readiness answer, as before).
