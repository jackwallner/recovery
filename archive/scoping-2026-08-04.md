# Recovery Countdown: positioning scope

Second-pass scoping, 2026-08-04. The `README.md` dossier (2026-08-01) settled the
*product mechanics*: HealthKit inputs, TRIMP-style load model, countdown plus Ready
marker, complication copy, free/Pro split. It did not test whether anyone searches
for this. This document covers demand, positioning, and the acquisition channel, and
lists the decisions that are Jack's to make.

Astro research app: `Recovery Countdown (pre-launch research)`, id `123`, 140
keywords tracked in `us`.

## Headline finding

**The recovery/readiness vocabulary has no measurable App Store search demand.**
Every keyword a user would type to find this app sits at Apple popularity 5, the
floor value that means "no measurable traffic". This is the same signature that
killed the eldercare vocabulary for Aging (all pop 5, graveyard of dead apps).

`recovery` at pop 9 is the only one above the floor, and its SERP is worthless:
the top 25 results for "recovery time" are **addiction recovery** (NA Recovery,
Clean Time, Sober Me, Sober Time at 40,462 ratings) and **file/photo recovery**
utilities. In App Store language, "recovery" means sobriety or deleted files. It
does not mean athletic recovery. That is a hard SERP-intent fail on the app's
current working name.

Apple Watch readiness apps; they arrive via brand search, category charts, YouTube
and Reddit, not via a longtail keyword. That is a different acquisition channel from
the one the rest of the fleet is built on.

## Fleet context that constrains this

From the fleet audit of `~/health`, `~/vitals`, and the 2026-07-25 growth/marketing
analyses:

- Fleet run rate is ~$510/month. Vitals alone is 42-49% of it. Nine of seventeen
  apps have real traffic and $0 revenue.
- Vitals' avg session is **28 seconds**, 3-4x/week: the widget and complication
  *are* the product, and 87% of deletions happen within 3 days. A glanceable
  countdown complication fits that proven shape better than almost anything else
  in the fleet.
- Lifetime one-time purchases are **64-68% of revenue** on the two apps that
  actually earn. Baseball has the fleet's best trial-to-paid at 31%.
- No app in the fleet supports paid UA: Apple Ads run $10-20 per paying user
  against a best-case $2.12 per download. Launch has to be organic.
- Architecture transfers cleanly. App Group + SwiftData cache written only by the
  phone, read by Watch app and complications (`vitals/Shared/Services/DataService.swift`,
  `health/Shared/Services/DataService.swift`); `HKObserverQuery` +
  `enableBackgroundDelivery` with debounced refresh and immediate `completionHandler()`
  on watchOS (`vitals/Shared/Services/HealthKitService.swift`); RevenueCat gated on
  any-active entitlement, never a hardcoded string (`revenuecat-entitlement-audit.md`
  found four apps whose named entitlement was already wrong and only worked because
  of that fallback).
- Genuinely new code: the countdown-to-a-future-timestamp complication timeline
  (every existing complication shows a cumulative daily value, not a decaying
  countdown), the `WorkoutRecord`/`RecoveryState`/`DailyContext` model, and the
  load calculator. Build the calculator as a pure function so it unit-tests the
  way `VO2MaxTests/CardioFreshnessTests` does.
- Standalone app, not a VO2 feature: VO2's loop is weeks-apart (a new personal
  best), Recovery's is hourly. Confirmed against VO2's actual scope; VO2 itself
  has no analytics yet, so there is no strength position from which to merge.

## Positioning options

### A. Ship it as scoped, accept the channel problem

Name and metadata built on `endurance` / `race day` / `athlete` plus brand terms
(`oura`, `polar`, `coros`), never on "recovery". Product stays the narrow countdown.
Cheapest path, reuses everything. Risk: joins the seven-app tier-2 graveyard, since
those apps' problem was distribution, not features.

### B. HYROX / hybrid-athlete recovery

`hyrox` is pop 41 at difficulty 17 — a 15-30x better ratio than anything else
available, and the only place in this space where a zero-authority app can rank on
day one. HYROX athletes train mixed strength and endurance several times a week and
have a genuine "can I go hard tomorrow" problem. The countdown is unchanged; the
framing, screenshots, and workout profiles change. Existing HYROX apps are timers,
race trackers and AI coaches (ROXFIT 3,867 ratings, RoxHype 50, TrainRox 41) — none
own recovery. Risk: HYROX is a trend that may cool, and the niche is small in
absolute terms.

### C. Fold into VO2 Max as "VO2+ Recovery"

No new app, no new ASO surface, no new review funnel. VO2 already owns `vo2 max`
(9/23) and reads workouts and RHR through `CardioContextService`. Risk: VO2's loop
is weeks-apart, and bolting a daily countdown onto it muddies both. VO2 also has no
traction data yet, so this is merging into an unknown.

### D. Sell against Garmin/WHOOP explicitly

"Garmin recovery time on your Apple Watch." Brand terms carry the only real volume
(`garmin` 65/59, `whoop` 64/69, `oura` 63/42, `coros` 50/43, `polar` 51/42) and
`oura`/`polar`/`coros` sit under the difficulty-50 ceiling. This is the frame the
Reddit demand evidence in the dossier actually uses. Risk: Apple metadata review
sometimes rejects competitor brand names in the keyword field, and the positioning
is parasitic on brands that could add the feature themselves.

### E. Don't build it

The keyword desert plus the seven-app zero-rating graveyard is the same evidence
pattern that correctly killed the eldercare vocabulary. Nine fleet apps already
have traffic and no revenue; fixing those is higher expected value than an
eighteenth app. Recovery becomes a research doc and a "VO2+ Heart Signal" feature.

## Decisions

Settled 2026-08-04. Written up in full in `positioning.md`.

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Positioning | **D** — Garmin/WHOOP on Apple Watch |
| Sport scope | Hybrid profiles per workout type (endurance / strength / mixed / easy) |
| Monetization | Fleet standard freemium, 7-day trial, monthly-yearly-lifetime |
| Channel | Brand-term ASO plus a comparison-led product page |

Still open:

1. Name, given "recovery" is a poisoned search term and Garmin/WHOOP cannot appear
   in the title or subtitle. Candidates in `positioning.md`.
2. Whether the countdown or the Ready marker is the hero on the Watch face.
3. Workout-type classification defaults for HYROX and CrossFit, which both arrive
   as `.functionalStrengthTraining` or `.highIntensityIntervalTraining`.
