A new recovery model: every session is scored on what it actually cost.

• The free countdown scores each session at your training level, instead of repeating your usual gap between sessions. That gap is still shown beside it.
• Countdowns now reach four days, so a half marathon or marathon reads longer than a long run.
• Intervals cost more than a steady run at the same average heart rate, and a long easy ride no longer outweighs a hard hour.
• Train again before a countdown ends and the two combine: the longer one in full, plus half of the shorter.
• Recharge+ adjusts a running countdown after each night's sleep, HRV, and resting heart rate.
• Complications keep updating through a four-day countdown and show the day it ends.
• Fixed: Ready now stays on screen after a long countdown, a workout that syncs just after you open the app is picked up straight away, and the Watch effort prompt opens properly.
