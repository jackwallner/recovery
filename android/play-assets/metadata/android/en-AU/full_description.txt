Know when to train again. Finish a qualifying workout and Recharge estimates a training-load recovery window, then counts it down to Ready.

Connect a compatible fitness app or wearable through Health Connect. Recharge reads shared workouts and heart rate. Optional sleep, resting heart rate, HRV (RMSSD), breathing rate, cardio fitness and weight add context. Health data stays on your device, and Recharge never writes to Health Connect.

• A countdown-complete notification when allowed

A compatible source must share records before Recharge can read them. One-minute heart rate recovery is unavailable. Wear OS and widgets are not included in this version.

Recharge provides a cardiovascular training estimate, not medical advice. It does not measure muscle repair, illness, injury risk or whether you are fit to train. Talk with a qualified health professional before making medical decisions.
